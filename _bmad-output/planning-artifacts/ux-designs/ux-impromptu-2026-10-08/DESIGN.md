## Brand & Style

Impromptu is a challenge generator that is also a show. The Cutout system gives it a look: one continuous collage, cut paper and liquid chrome, Bodoni headlines against tiny tracked Y2K labels. The posture is **editorial restraint from the collage posters, brightness from Y2K**. It is playful and tactile, and never cute. Pieces sit askew the way a person would glue them. Chrome glints at the edges, and the instruction in the middle is always flat, horizontal, and easy to read.

Cutout is a style mockup only. Its name, labels, page structure, and Reveal mechanics are not binding. This file keeps Cutout's tokens verbatim and adds Impromptu-specific deltas where the PRD's filming rules require them (the Stage type ramp, the safe area, the two extra reveal pieces). All UI copy shown in these specs is **working copy**. Final copy is still open with the founder.

The guiding rule comes from Cutout: **build pages as a journey, not a stack.** It has one exception. The Challenge Stage is a held, still frame because it is the shot.

No mockups, wireframes, or imports were produced for this run. These tables and prose are the whole visual contract, and they win over the Cutout artifact on any conflict.

Verified pairs come from Cutout's token notes and were re-measured for this spine. Pairs not listed here are unverified and must not carry text.

- **Grape (`{colors.grape}`)** is the signature electric purple. It is used for the orbit thread, four-point stars, the focus ring, and the "on" state of switches and checkboxes. It is a fill only behind cream text at 24px or larger (4.9:1). **Grape-deep (`{colors.grape-deep}`)** carries purple as type: the Y2K headline on sun, the grape ticker, and the rep-done stamp.
- **Sun gradient (`{colors.sun-light}` → `{colors.sun}` → `{colors.sun-deep}`)** marks the one primary action per view (the sun button) and the Difficulty Dial face. Ink on the darkest stop holds 7.1:1.
- **Vermilion (`{colors.vermilion}`)** is for display only (24px+, 3.9:1 on paper): red dots, italic heading words, ornaments. Small red text uses `{colors.vermilion-ink}`. Constraint ink uses `{colors.stamp}` (5.5:1 on paper, 5.7:1 on cream).
- **Decorative-only tokens:** `bust-*`, `foil-*` (they carry plum text on the foil slip, 10.5:1 or better), and `neon-*` (vertical bars on lilac). These never encode meaning.
- **Focus** is a 2px ring at a 3px offset, drawn on the ground (never on a control's own fill). The ring color depends on the ground under it:
  - `{colors.focus}` (grape) on night 3.3:1, lilac 3.4:1, and paper 4.7:1.
  - `{colors.focus-on-sun}` (ink) on every sun stop: 7.1:1 or better.
  - `{colors.focus-on-lilac-deep}` (grape-deep) on lilac-deep: 4.1:1.
  - Controls never sit on `{colors.night-glow}`, where grape measures 2.9:1.
  - On the Stage, the lilac-deep edge fade begins at least 120px from the corner controls, so back and sound sit on plain lilac.
- **Grain** is one overlay across the whole page at 0.1 opacity (overlay blend). It is suppressed inside the Stage safe area and behind any essential text.
- **No dark mode.** Grounds are fixed by position in the journey, and `prefers-color-scheme` does not change them. Night already carries every page's footer.

## Typography

Three families, each with one job (Cutout):

- **Bodoni Moda, the editorial voice.** Used for the wordmark (setup hero only), section `display` headlines (lowercase, with the second line in italic), and every **reveal word**: Topic, Style (italic), and the Skill and Medium values. PRD §6 requires display type on reveal words.
- **Unbounded, the Y2K voice.** Used for `meta` labels (uppercase, tracked, never sentences), buttons, stamp lettering, index numbers, the countdown, and one `y2k-display` headline per page.
- **Instrument Sans, the reading voice.** Used for Briefs, ledes, body copy, form labels, and Reflection text.

Hierarchy works by contrast: huge serif against tiny tracked meta, with the middle sizes skipped. Headlines are lowercase sentence fragments that end with a full stop.

**Stage ramp (filming minimums, FR-31).** Cutout's Brief size (21px) is too small to film, so the Stage uses its own tokens:

| Role | Desktop | Phone (≤ 860px) |
|---|---|---|
| Brief | `{typography.brief-stage}` 32/44 | `{typography.brief-stage-phone}` 24/32 |
| Topic | `{typography.topic-stage}` 48; drops to `{typography.topic-stage-long}` 32 when the value runs past two lines | `{typography.topic-stage-phone}` 32 |
| Style | `{typography.style-stage}` 32 italic | `{typography.style-stage-phone}` 24 italic |
| Constraint | `{typography.stamp-stage}` 24 uppercase | `{typography.stamp-stage-phone}` 20 uppercase |
| Skill / Medium | `{typography.tab-value-stage}` 28 | `{typography.tab-value-stage-phone}` 22 |
| Piece label | `{typography.piece-label}` 13 | `{typography.piece-label-phone}` 12 |
| Countdown | `{typography.countdown}` 56 | `{typography.countdown-phone}` 40 |
| Level / mode meta | `{typography.stage-meta}` 14 | `{typography.stage-meta-phone}` 12 |

On desktop no Input value falls below 24px and the Brief never falls below 32px. Type never shrinks below these minimums. Spacing compresses first (see Layout), and the column scrolls only as a last resort.

**Essential text is always horizontal (PRD §6).** Pieces keep their collage tilt (scrap -1.2°, foil 4°, stamp -7°, tabs ±1.5°), but each piece's label and value are counter-rotated to 0°, so every Input reads level. The material tilts and the words do not. Long values wrap with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` and `hyphens: auto`.

`{typography.brief-setup}` (Cutout's 21px brief) is for the static sample on Setup section 02 only. It is never used on the Stage. Setup-only styles: `wordmark`, `display`, `display-setup`, `display-phone`, `topic`, `card-title`, `style-italic`, `numeral`, `y2k-display`, `index-number`, `stamp`, `caps-intro`.

Fonts are self-hosted variable WOFF2 files (Bodoni Moda 400–900 with italic, Unbounded 200–900, Instrument Sans 400–700) with `font-display: swap`. Fallbacks follow Cutout: Didot / "Bodoni 72" / serif, "Arial Black" / sans-serif, and "Helvetica Neue" / sans-serif.

## Layout & Spacing

The spacing scale is Cutout's: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 56 / 80 / 120. Content max width is `{spacing.content-max}`, desktop side padding is `{spacing.14}`, the header inset is `{spacing.header-inset}`, and the phone gutter is `{spacing.gutter-phone}`.

**Setup page, desktop (≥ 1280px).** The page is a vertical journey:

1. **01 Night hero** is an asymmetric two-column layout. The left column (about 46%, roughly 590px at 1280) holds the setup stack. The right column holds the hero art: the `hero-bloom-orange` portrait plus the three.js chrome piece, with the wordmark behind them. **Get a challenge must be visible without scrolling at 1280×800.** The stack's height budget is:

   | Row | Content | Height |
   |---|---|---|
   | Header | Brand mark, Practice link | 64 |
   | Headline | Two lines of `{typography.display-setup}` | 130 |
   | Explanation | One line of `{typography.lede}`, plus gap | 44 |
   | Dial row | 160px dial; to its right, the Level description and, at Perform, the timing segmented control | 180 |
   | Mediums | Label, then four chips on one row | 72 |
   | Selects | "This time" select field and Skill select field side by side, with labels; Skill info button beside its select | 72 |
   | Action row | Quick reveal switch and the sun button | 52 |
   | Gaps | | 104 |
   | **Total** | | **≈ 718 of 800** |

   At 1440 and wider, the headline may step up to `{typography.display}`.
2. **02 Lilac** shows what is in a Challenge. A static sample composition (CL-5 example 2) appears with labels, next to the six Skill descriptions.
3. **03 Paper** shows the four Levels and the loop: three `poster-card`s (reveal / make / reflect) staggered 0 / 90 / 40px.
4. **04 Sun** is the closing call: one `y2k-display` line and a second Get a challenge.
5. **Night footer** holds the email signup, Practice and Privacy links, and the storage note.

Grounds fade across `{spacing.seam-fade-min}`–`{spacing.seam-fade-max}`, never butted edge to edge. Tickers and crossing pieces sit within ±`{spacing.30}` of each seam.

**Challenge Stage.** The Stage is a single lilac ground. Lilac is the committed default; if it ever changes, every Stage color pair must be re-measured. Content sits in a centered **safe-area column** `{spacing.safe-area-width}` wide (about 600px at 1080px tall, 450px at 800px) and the full viewport height. The 9/16 rule always wins on desktop; there is no minimum width above 860px. Inside the column, from top to bottom:

1. The Stage mark, plus the Level and mode meta (and RETRY or VARIATION when it applies).
2. Skill and Medium ticket tabs, side by side.
3. The Topic paper scrap, full column width, with the Style foil slip tucked over its top-right corner and the Constraint stamp pressed onto its lower right.
4. The Brief.
5. The Time Limit or countdown.
6. The action row: one sun button, with secondary line buttons beside it.

Between groups the spacing is `{spacing.stage-gap}`, and between the composition and the actions it is `{spacing.8}`. The back control and the sound/mute control sit in the viewport corners at `{spacing.header-inset}`. They are outside the safe area on purpose, so a 9:16 crop drops them. Decoration may only occupy the space outside the column.

**Fit rule.** The Held and Creating states must fit without scrolling at 1280×800 for any Brief up to `{spacing.stage-brief-max-chars}` characters (a library content cap, CL-3) `[ASSUMPTION]`. When the composition does not fit the viewport, these steps apply in order until it does:

1. Gaps drop to `{spacing.stage-gap-compact}`, and the composition-to-actions gap drops to `{spacing.6}`.
2. Topic drops to `{typography.topic-stage-long}`.
3. The countdown caption moves inline, left of the digits.
4. The countdown drops to `{typography.countdown-phone}`.
5. The column scrolls. Nothing clips, and type never goes below the minimums.

The worst case at 1280×800 is a 160-character timed Brief. After steps 1–4 it measures about 706px of 800: mark and meta 32, tabs 64, scrap 148, Brief about 220, countdown 40, actions 52, gaps 96, top and bottom margins 64. A 1280×800 or taller window is the filming baseline.

**Piece anatomy and keep-outs.** Each Input value has a text box, which is a keep-out: nothing overlaps it, including the foil slip, the stamp, lock toggles, the rep-done stamp, and decoration. The paper scrap has three zones: a label row, the Topic text box, and a 56px bottom band. The Constraint stamp sits in the right half of the band and the rep-done stamp in the left half. The foil slip overlaps only the scrap's top-right corner outside the Topic text box; the scrap reserves 40% of its top row for it. Lock toggles sit on each piece's outer left edge, centered vertically, overlapping only the piece border.

**Mid-size widths (861–1279px).** Setup stacks the hero art behind the controls at reduced size, under a night scrim of 0.85 or more. The Stage is unchanged, and decoration narrows to the visible margins.

**Practice page.** A night header band holds the brand mark, a back-to-setup link, and the page title. The paper body holds the Practice Map (three count rows) above the Practice History list, a single column at most 760px wide, with export and clear controls at the top right of the history.

**Phone (≤ 860px).** One column with a `{spacing.gutter-phone}` gutter. Chrome, neon bars, posters, and the orbit thread are hidden. Hero art is masked into night beneath the setup controls, and the controls sit on solid night. On the Stage, the column is the full width minus gutters, with no minimum width, so it works at 320px. The scrap's bottom band grows to hold the stamp below the Topic text, so the stamp is still printed on paper. The action row becomes a bottom bar fixed to the column's bottom edge, within thumb reach. Grounds and type keep their roles.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical, not interface depth. Shadows are soft and never hard offsets:

- `0 10px 14px rgba(40,25,10,0.22)` (lift-soft) for pieces, popovers, and ticket tabs on light grounds.
- `0 18px 22px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)` (lift) for chrome and cutouts on night or saturated grounds.
- `0 30px 40px -24px rgba(23,19,17,0.5)` (poster) for poster cards and rep cards, deepening on hover to `0 46px 60px -30px rgba(23,19,17,0.6)`.
- `0 10px 30px -8px rgba(255,140,20,0.55)` (sun glow) under the sun button only.
- `0 18px 40px -18px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` under seam tickers.

**Cursor depth** (setup hero only) moves each layer by its depth value times the pointer offset, eased over 900ms. The values are -12px for far layers (sun discs, grids), 10px for type, 22px for hero art, and 40px for chrome. The three.js chrome piece takes the nearest depth. **The Stage has no parallax.** During the shuffle, the three.js layer animates behind and around the safe area and then freezes on its last frame once the Challenge is held.

Layering order, back to front: ground → grain → orbit thread → decoration and 3D → content → controls → popover and dialog.

## Shapes

Shapes follow collage logic. Paper is nearly square-cornered (`{rounded.scrap}` 2px). Foil and stamps have hard edges (`{rounded.none}`). Pills are reserved for buttons, chips, and segmented controls (`{rounded.full}`). Circles are for sun discs, red dots, the dial, lock toggles, and Stage icon buttons (`{rounded.disc}`). Text fields and dialogs use 2px. No other radii appear. Torn edges come from the paper-scrap texture and its mask, never from `border-radius`.

## Components

Visual specs only. Behavior lives in `EXPERIENCE.md` → Component Patterns.

**Actions**
- **Sun button.** The one primary action per view. It is a sun-gradient pill with an inner top highlight, the sun glow, a `{typography.button-sun}` uppercase label, and a trailing arrow. Hover lifts it 2px over 250ms and sweeps a highlight across it. Pressed, it sinks 1px. Disabled, it shows a flat `{colors.cream-dim}` fill with `{colors.ink-soft}` text and no glow. Focus is a 2px grape ring at a 3px offset on the ground.
- **Ink button.** `{colors.ink}` pill with a `{colors.cream}` label, for the secondary strong action ("Back to your challenge", Export, the email signup on night). On night it gets a 1px `{colors.cream-dim}` outline so its edge holds 8.6:1.
- **Line button.** A 1px outline pill in the ground's text color, for Reroll, Discard, Pause, and Change it again. On hover it fills at 8% of the text color.
- **Stage icon button.** A 52px circle with a 1px `{colors.plum-muted}` outline and a plum glyph, for back (arrow-left) and sound (speaker, or speaker with slash). The sound control shows its state in the glyph and in a `{typography.stage-meta}` caption ("SOUND OFF"). It is never conveyed by color alone.

**Setup controls (on night)**
- **Difficulty Dial.** A 160px sun-gradient disc with a `{colors.grape-deep}` pointer notch. Four position labels in `{typography.button}` cream sit **outside** the disc on night, around its upper arc (16.0:1). The active label gets a 2px cream underline and a leading grape star, so the selection never depends on color alone. The Level description sits to the dial's right in `{typography.body}` cream-dim. The focus ring circles the whole dial on night. On phones it becomes a horizontal four-stop track: a 4px `{colors.cream-dim}` track (8.9:1 on night), a 28px sun-gradient thumb with a 1.5px ink border, and stop labels below the track.
- **Chip toggle (Mediums).** A pill with a cream outline when off. When on, it is a cream fill with ink text plus a leading tick glyph, so state never depends on fill alone.
- **Select field.** A cream-outlined pill showing the current value ("This time: Random", "Skill: Random") with a chevron. It opens the native option list. Used for "This time" (Random plus each enabled Medium) and Skill focus (Random plus the six Skills), where segments would not fit.
- **Segmented control.** One bordered pill track, used only for Perform timing (Timed / Untimed / Either). The selected segment is a cream fill with ink text plus a leading tick glyph.
- **Switch (Quick reveal).** A pill track, `{colors.cream-dim}` when off and `{colors.grape}` when on. The cream thumb has a 1.5px ink border, so it holds 8.6:1 against the off track. An ON or OFF word sits beside the track, and the visible text label sits before it.
- **Info button.** An 18px "i" glyph in a 1.5px ring, inside a 52px hit area, opening a cream `popover`.
- **Motion toggle** (Setup and Practice headers). A small line button in `{typography.meta}` reading "MOTION ON" or "MOTION OFF", with a play or pause glyph.

**On the Stage**

Each of the five reveal pieces (two ticket tabs, the paper scrap, the foil slip, and the ink stamp) carries a visible `{typography.piece-label}` (SKILL, MEDIUM, TOPIC, STYLE, CONSTRAINT). The material treatment reinforces the label and never replaces it. Labels and values are counter-rotated to horizontal inside every tilted piece (see Typography).

- **Ticket tab (Skill, Medium).** A cream printed card, `{rounded.scrap}`, with a perforated left edge (a dotted 1px rule), tilted ±1.5°. The label is in vermilion-ink and the value in `{typography.tab-value-stage}` ink.
- **Paper scrap (Topic).** A real torn-paper texture (`paper-scrap.webp`) tilted -1.2°, with lift-soft shadow. The label is in vermilion-ink and the value in `{typography.topic-stage}` ink at 13.5:1. Behind the text box the texture is contrast-flattened (luminance variance within ±4%), so no fine texture sits behind essential text.
- **Foil slip (Style).** A thin iridescent strip (foil-a → d at 120°) tilted 4° and tucked over the scrap's top-right corner. The value is in `{typography.style-stage}` italic plum. The foil shimmers through its gradient (6s, alternate) only while the Reveal is running, and the shimmer stops when held.
- **Ink stamp (Constraint).** A double-rule rectangle in `{colors.stamp}`, tilted -7°, with uppercase `{typography.stamp-stage}` lettering. It is always printed on the paper scrap's bottom band (5.5:1 or better), never on lilac, where it would measure 3.9:1. The `ink-mask.png` wear applies to the double rule only. The label and lettering are unworn.
- **Brief block.** Plain `{typography.brief-stage}` plum directly on lilac, max 34ch, left-aligned, with no texture, tilt, or decoration.
- **Empty slot.** Each piece's final footprint as a 1px dashed `{colors.plum-muted}` outline with its label inside, at the same tilt as the piece it will hold.
- **Lock toggle.** A 52px disc on each piece's outer left edge. Unlocked, it shows an outline padlock in plum-muted (5.9:1). Locked, it is an ink disc with a cream padlock, and a "LOCKED" caption in `{typography.piece-label}` plum (9.3:1) appears beside it. It appears only in the held state before Start creating.
- **Countdown.** `{typography.countdown}` with tabular numerals (mm:ss) in plum, and a `{typography.stage-meta}` caption above it ("TIME LIMIT 5 MIN" before start, "PAUSED" when paused). At zero, the digits are replaced by "time's up." in `{typography.card-title}` plum. There is no red and no flashing.
- **Rep-done stamp.** A grape-deep double-rule stamp ("REP DONE", working copy) with its frame tilted 6° and its lettering level. It is pressed into the left half of the scrap's bottom band (7.3:1 on paper) and never covers the Topic or the Brief.
- **Variation option.** In the Variation pick state, each changeable piece gets a 1px dashed plum-muted outline 6px outside it, with a "CHANGE" caption in plum. Selected, the outline becomes 3px solid grape-deep (5.2:1 on lilac) and the caption reads "CHANGE THIS" with a filled radio dot. Pieces that cannot change get no outline.
- **Reflection panel.** A cream panel with a 1px ink border, replacing the action row inside the safe area. It holds two stacked text fields and the sun "Save rep" button. The composition above compresses (fit-rule steps 1–2) to make room. Below 900px tall, the Brief stays visible and the pieces may scroll out of view.
- **Next steps.** The sun **Try another version** button runs full column width, with **Retry** and **Get a challenge** as line buttons side by side below it, a `{spacing.3}` gap between them.
- **Stage mark.** "impromptu" in `{typography.stage-mark}` plum, with a 14px grape four-point star.

**Forms and feedback**
- **Text field** (email, Reflection). A cream field with a 1px ink-soft border and a 2px radius, a `{typography.label}` label above it, and helper or counter text in `{typography.meta}` below. In error, the border becomes 2px `{colors.vermilion-ink}`, a red-dot marker appears, and the message is written out in text.
- **Consent checkbox.** A 24px box with a 1.5px border, unticked by default. Checked, it fills with grape and shows a cream tick. The label sits beside it in `{typography.body}`.
- **Notice banner.** A cream panel with a 1px ink border, used for "challenge in progress", "challenge waiting", and "history not kept". It holds a text line and one or two buttons.
- **Inline message.** Ground-colored text with a leading 10px vermilion dot. Used for Lock conflicts, the last-Medium rule, and form errors.
- **Email signup.** Two variants:
  - **Night (footer):** a cream field, cream labels and consent text, a cream-bordered checkbox, and an ink button with a cream-dim outline.
  - **Lilac (Stage, after a saved Rep):** a cream card with a 1px ink border, ink labels, and a line button.
  - **Both:** fields stack vertically with `{spacing.3}` gaps. The privacy link sits under the consent line. Errors appear under the field they concern. Success replaces the form in place with one line of `{typography.body}` text.
- **Dialog** (Clear all data). A paper panel with a `rgba(18,16,16,0.72)` night scrim, an ink title in `{typography.card-title}`, an ink "Clear everything" button, and a line "Keep my data" button.
- **Popover** (Skill info). A cream panel with an ink `{typography.body}` description, at most 280px wide, using lift-soft.

**Practice**
- **Rep card.** A cream card on paper with the poster shadow. It holds a meta row in `{typography.meta}` (SKILL · MEDIUM · LEVEL · DATE · TIMED 4:12), the Brief in `{typography.lede}` ink, and the Reflection answers in `{typography.body}` ink-soft under small Unbounded question labels. A RETRY or VARIATION tag is an outline pill.
- **Practice Map cell.** A count in `{typography.index-number}` ink above a `{typography.meta}` label. Zero shows an em dash in ink-soft. There are no bars, percentages, rings, or fill-to-goal visuals.

**Journey furniture** (setup and practice only, never on the Stage)
- **Setup header.** A night band inset `{spacing.header-inset}`: the Stage mark at left (cream on night), and the Practice link and Motion toggle at right in `{typography.meta}` cream.
- **Section header.** A numbered meta line on the left (`01 — IMPROMPTU`), the hairline-diamond ornament in the center, and a three-line meta stack on the right.
- **Ticker.** Bands that cross the night/lilac seam (sun gradient, ink text, -2.4°) and the paper/sun seam (grape-deep, cream text, 2°). They carry real Topic and Style fragments from the library in `{typography.style-italic}`, separated by chrome bursts. They are decoration, so screen readers skip them.
- **Poster card.** A full-bleed poster image in a 2px frame, with the title in `{typography.card-title}` and the last word in vermilion italic.
- **Orbit thread.** One 2.5px grape line that runs from the hero orbit ring through the margins and gutters to the footer globe. It never crosses text.
- **Chrome and cutouts.** Two to four pieces per screen, never behind text, hidden on phones. The three.js hero piece counts as one of them.
- **Footer.** A night ground with the email signup at the left, links and the storage note at the right, and the cream wordmark in `{typography.display}`.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Keep every Input, the Brief, the countdown, and the primary action inside the Stage safe area | Let chrome, stars, neon, or the orbit thread enter the safe area |
| Use the material piece and a visible label together | Rely on paper, foil, or stamp alone to say which Input is which |
| Set every reveal word in Bodoni Moda | Set the Brief in Bodoni or Unbounded |
| Keep essential text horizontal; pieces may tilt up to ±7° | Rotate the Brief, the countdown, or any control label |
| Use one sun button per view | Put two sun buttons side by side, or put sun-gradient fill behind body text |
| Use grape fill only behind cream text at 24px+ | Use grape or vermilion for small text |
| Stop all motion once the Challenge is held | Keep the foil shimmer, tickers, bob, or 3D running on a held Stage |
| Give state a glyph or word as well as a color | Show lock, sound, toggle, or error state by color alone |
| Show Practice Map counts as plain numerals | Show bars, rings, percentages, levels-up, or streak flames |
| Use the three.js layer for decoration only | Render any text, label, or control in WebGL |
| Show the static 2D fallback for reduced motion or missing WebGL | Leave an empty hole where the 3D piece would be |
| Keep artwork original (Higgsfield or drawn) | Use the reference posters themselves |
